Why Knox is different

Every AI homework app promises "answers." Here's what we actually did differently.

💚

Free gets the same quality as paid

Most apps stick free users on a lite version to push you to upgrade. Knox gives you the full AI on day one. Paying only removes the usage limit — it doesn't unlock better answers.

⚡

No modes to pick. Just ask.

Other apps make you choose "Answer mode" or "Solver mode" or "Tutor mode" before you even type. Knox reads what you actually need from how you ask. Say "quiz me" and it teaches. Say "just tell me" and it tells you.

💬

Talks like a tutor, not a template

You won't get "Final Answer:" / "Explanation:" / "Key Points:" like it's a filled-out form. Knox answers the way a smart friend would — direct, natural, with real reasoning. No filler.

📷

Snap a photo, done

Point your camera at a worksheet, whiteboard, textbook, or your own handwriting. Knox reads it, confirms what it sees so you know it got the right problem, then helps.

Just ask however you want

No modes, no menus. Type the way you'd actually ask a friend — Knox reads what you need. Real examples:

⚡ Solve it
You say

"What's the derivative of 3x² + 5x − 2?"

Knox says

The derivative is 6x + 5. Power rule — bring the exponent down, subtract one. Constants disappear.

🧠 Teach me
You say

"Don't just give me the answer — teach me how."

Knox says

Sounds good. Start with 3x² — there's a rule for x-to-a-power terms. What do you think it becomes?

✓ Check my work
You say

"I got 6x + 5x. Is that right?"

Knox says

Close — one slip. First term's right. But 5x becomes 5, not 5x — x¹ turns into x⁰ = 1.

Common questions

Quick answers to what most people ask before signing up.

How is Knox different from ChatGPT or other AI tools? +
Knox is built specifically for students, not a generic chatbot. It reads how you ask — a straight question gets a direct answer, but say "quiz me instead" and Knox switches to teaching mode on the spot. No menus, no modes to pick, and a full chat history so you never lose your work.
Will I be charged after the 7-day free trial? +
Yes — unless you cancel before the trial ends. The free trial on Knox Plus Monthly gives you 7 days of full access. After that, $9.99/month is charged automatically. You can cancel anytime from your account page (we'll show you how), and you'll keep access until the period you paid for ends. No surprise fees.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yep. Sign in, open your profile menu, choose "Account & Plan," then "Manage Billing" — that takes you to a secure Stripe page where one click cancels. You'll keep using Knox until the end of your current billing period, no questions asked.
What subjects does Knox cover? +
Every subject you'd see in K-12 through college: math (algebra, geometry, calculus, stats), all sciences (biology, chemistry, physics), history, English/essays, foreign languages, economics, computer science, and more. If your teacher assigns it, Knox can help with it.
Can I upload a photo of my homework? +
Yes — Knox can read photos of handwritten or printed homework. Just tap the camera icon in the app and snap a photo. Knox will read it and answer it just like a typed question. Free users draw photos from the same daily credit pool as questions (a photo just uses a bit more of it); Knox Plus gets unlimited.
Is this just cheating? +
Knox defaults to a straight, real answer — useful when you're stuck and just need to move on. But if you'd rather be taught than told, just say so: "walk me through it" or "quiz me instead" switches Knox into guiding you there yourself, one hint at a time, instead of handing over the solution.
How do I access Knox? +
Knox lives at knoxknowsapp.com/app. Sign up free and get 10 questions to start, then 5 more every day (save up to 20) — the same quality as paid. Upgrade to Knox Plus for truly unlimited.
Does Knox remember my previous questions? +
Yes — Knox saves your chat history in the sidebar so you can always go back and review previous answers. Your history is saved to your account.
Do you offer refunds? +
Yes — 30-day money-back guarantee on any paid plan (monthly or yearly). You can cancel anytime to stop future renewals. Email support@knoxknowsapp.com and we'll take care of it. See our full refund policy.
What if Knox gets something wrong? +
Knox is highly accurate but not perfect — like any tutor. Always double-check important answers against your notes or textbook, especially for graded work. If you spot something off, hit the 👎 button below the response and we'll use that to make Knox smarter. Direct feedback also welcome at support@knoxknowsapp.com.

Still have questions? Email support@knoxknowsapp.com — Sion (a real human) reads every message.
