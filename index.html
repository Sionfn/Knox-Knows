A LITTLE HELP. A BIG AHA.
Why does 2x + 6 = 14 give x = 4?
KNOX Example

Think of an equation as a balanced scale. Do the same thing to both sides.

2x + 6 = 14
Subtract 6 → 2x = 8
Divide by 2 → x = 4

Check it: 2 × 4 + 6 = 14. Balanced!

FROM “I'M STUCK” TO “I SEE IT” · EXAMPLE
I got x = 10. Where did I go wrong?
KNOX

Let's check the step where you moved the 6. To keep the equation balanced, subtract 6 from both sides—not just one.

2x + 6 − 6 = 14 − 6
2x = 8

What happens when you divide both sides by 2?

Show your attempt so Knox can focus on the part you need help with.

A GUIDED CONVERSATION · EXAMPLE
KNOX

We need to solve 3x + 5 = 20. What could we undo first to get the x-term on its own?

Subtract 5 from both sides?

Exactly. That leaves 3x = 15. What operation undoes multiplying by 3?

Divide by 3. So x = 5!

Your thinking stays at the center of the conversation.

A FEW MORE THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is Knox Knows?

Knox Knows is an AI study companion with direct explanations in Ask Knox, guided practice in Learn, and study guides, flashcards, and quizzes in Study Hub. You can type a question or attach a photo.

How is Learn different from Ask Knox?

Ask Knox explains your question directly. Learn guides you with hints and questions so you can work through the next step yourself. Choose Ask for an explanation, or Learn when you want to practice your reasoning.

How do free credits work?

New free accounts start with 10 credits. Your balance replenishes by 5 each day, up to 20. A text question costs 1 credit and a photo question costs 2. Starting a Learn session costs 1 credit and includes up to 14 guided follow-ups within that session.

What does unlimited mean on Knox Plus?

Plus removes the free credit balance for questions and photos. Fair-use protections still apply to prevent automated or excessive abuse; unlimited does not mean unrestricted automated use.

Can I change how Knox explains things?

Yes. In Settings, choose standard, visual, step-by-step, or concise explanations. Visual explanations can include examples and text diagrams. You can also select English or Spanish for AI answers. Interface translations are still being expanded.

Can I use Knox on my phone?

Yes. The website adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops. You can type a question or upload a clear photo. There is also a Knox Knows extension for Chrome.

Will Knox always be right?

No. AI can misunderstand a photo or make a mistake. Check important answers, compare the reasoning with your class materials, and ask follow-up questions. Use Knox to understand your work and follow your school's rules.

How do subscriptions and support work?

Plus is $9.99 monthly or $79.99 billed yearly. Eligible new monthly subscriptions include a 3-day trial; the paid subscription starts afterward unless you cancel. Manage billing in Settings, review the refund policy below, or email support@knoxknowsapp.com for help.
